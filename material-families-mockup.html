What changed from v1

Your model was better than mine in two places.

No modes

Organise and Clean Up were tabs. They're gone — both are just buckets in the same dropdown, so you can pull a bad variant straight out of a family and drop it on the disabled pile without changing screens.

Swap, don't re-pick

Empty twelve families into the holding pen, hit ⇄, and now the pen is on the left and you're feeding it back out. Same two slots, opposite direction.

The holding pen writes nothing

Your "take everything out first" raised a real question — where does a variant live while it's out of a family? Answer: it doesn't move at all. The pen is a basket. Nothing is written until a variant lands in a real family, so you can empty every family you own and still walk away having changed nothing.

Delete lives where the dead things are

Load Disabled · legacy into a slot and the rows grow a status column and the pane grows a Delete button. It isn't a separate screen because it isn't a separate job.

Not in a family

Pulling the last variant out of a family leaves an empty shell. Delete it, deactivate it, or leave it for you to decide later?

Not this screen's job

Vendors, part numbers, files, colour management, lot tracking — that's the material detail page. Click a family name here and it opens there. This screen only does bulk moves and family-wide corrections.
